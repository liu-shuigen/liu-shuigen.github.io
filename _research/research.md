My research focuses on analysis and development of algorithms for high-dimensional sampling and uncertainty quantification, with applications in Bayesian inference, inverse problems, and generative modeling.
I am currently developing *localization* methods for sampling that utilize graphical locality to reduce computational and statistical complexity.
I also work on ensemble Kalman methods for high-dimensional inference and data assimilation.
I am broadly interested in stochastic algorithms for high-dimensional problems.
